# Privacy Policy & Support – Nutraxis

**Last updated:** November 2026

Nutraxis is a health and wellness application designed to provide personalized nutrition insights and habit-tracking features to support healthier daily choices.

---

## Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Nutraxis may collect personal data, including health and wellness information you choose to provide, in order to:

- Provide core app functionality  
- Personalize wellness and nutrition insights  
- Improve app performance and user experience  

Nutraxis does **not provide medical diagnoses or treatment**, and nothing in the app should be treated as medical advice.

All data is handled securely and in accordance with Apple App Store policies.  
We do **not sell personal data to third parties**, and we do **not use health-related data for advertising or marketing purposes**.

Subscriptions and purchases are managed securely through **Apple In-App Purchases**.

---

## Data We May Collect

To provide the features of the application, Nutraxis may collect information such as:

- Account information (for example, email address if you create an account)
- Nutrition and wellness information you voluntarily enter (meals, water intake, weight, activity data)
- Health-related information you choose to provide, such as medical conditions (for example diabetes, cholesterol, or bone health), injuries, and other context used to personalize nutrition and workout guidance
- Photos you take of meals for food recognition
- Voice recordings, where you use voice-based food logging
- Health and fitness data from Apple HealthKit, where you grant permission (such as steps and active calories), used to show your activity alongside your nutrition data
- Usage analytics to improve app performance and user experience
- Device information necessary for app functionality

This information is used only to provide the core functionality of the app and improve the user experience, and is provided voluntarily by you — Nutraxis does not access health or medical records from any other source.

**Apple Health data:** Health and fitness data obtained through Apple HealthKit is used solely to provide app features (such as showing your activity and calories burned) and is never used for advertising, marketing, or other data-mining purposes, and is never sold or disclosed to data brokers, advertising platforms, or other information resellers.

---

## AI Features

Nutraxis uses artificial intelligence (AI) technologies, including third-party AI providers such as **OpenAI**, to analyze food photos and voice recordings, generate nutrition insights, and provide wellness guidance through the AI Coach.

Food photos, voice recordings, and the nutrition/activity context needed to generate a response are sent to these AI providers solely to produce that result, and are processed under their own data-handling terms.

AI responses are provided for informational purposes and may not always be accurate.

Users should not rely on AI-generated insights as medical or professional advice.

---

## Third-Party Services

Nutraxis may use trusted third-party services to operate certain features of the application, including:

- **Firebase / Google Cloud** for authentication, secure data storage, and backend processing
- **OpenAI** (and similar AI providers) for food photo analysis, voice transcription, and AI Coach responses
- **RevenueCat** for managing subscriptions
- **Apple In-App Purchases** for subscription and payment processing
- **Open Food Facts** for barcode product lookups

These services may process limited information required to provide their functionality.  
Nutraxis does not control how these third-party services handle data, and their own privacy policies may apply.

---

## Your Rights

**If you are in the European Economic Area or UK (GDPR):** you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Where we process health-related data, we rely on your explicit consent, which you may withdraw at any time by deleting that information in the app or deleting your account. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA):** you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Nutraxis does not sell or share personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app account deletion feature or contact us using the support email below.

---

## Data Retention & Deletion

User data is stored only as long as necessary to provide the services of the application.

You can permanently delete your account and data at any time from within the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account). This immediately and permanently deletes your stored data — including meals, activity logs, weight history, workout history, and any medical/health information you provided — along with your account itself.

You may also request deletion by contacting the developer using the support email below.

---

## Children's Privacy

Nutraxis is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us using the support email below so we can delete it.

---

## Health Disclaimer

Nutraxis is intended for **general wellness and informational purposes only**.

It is **not a medical application** and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making health-related decisions.
